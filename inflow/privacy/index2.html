01. Overview

Inflow ("Inflow," "we," "us," or "our") is a digital note-taking application developed by Beckett Graham. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Inflow app, create an account, purchase a subscription, or use Inflow AI.

We aim to collect only the information needed to provide, maintain, secure, and improve the functions you choose to use. This policy applies to the Inflow app and its associated account and AI services.

02. Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, Inflow and its service providers may collect or process the following categories of information.

Data typeExamples and purpose
Email addressAn email address, including an Apple private relay address, associated with sign-in and your account.

Basic technical data necessary to service network requests, such as IP addresses, timestamps, request identifiers, and error information, may also be processed by our infrastructure and security providers.

Notebook content stored on your device

Inflow's initial release stores notebooks and imported documents locally on your device. Simply writing or importing a file does not mean your entire notebook library is uploaded to our AI service. Selected content is transmitted when you use an online feature that requires it, such as Inflow AI.

Local storage reminder: Inflow 1.0 does not provide automatic iCloud notebook syncing. Removing the app or losing your device may result in lost local notes. Export important notebooks regularly.

03. How we use information

We use collected information to authenticate accounts, maintain subscriptions and access to paid features, apply AI usage limits, process AI requests you initiate, provide customer support, prevent misuse, and operate the service reliably.

Inflow does not sell your personal information or use it for third-party advertising or cross-app advertising tracking. If our practices change, we will update this policy and any required App Store privacy disclosures.

04. Inflow AI

When you make a request to Inflow AI, the text, images, or selected notebook and document content included in that request are transmitted through our Cloudflare-based service to OpenAI to generate an answer. Do not submit information you do not want processed by an external AI provider.

Your AI requests and responses may be processed and retained by service providers according to their data handling and safety practices. OpenAI states that API content is not used to train its models by default, unless the API customer opts in; its standard abuse-monitoring logs may be retained for up to 30 days, and certain API features have separate application-state retention rules.

Using Inflow AI is optional. You can continue taking and editing local notes without submitting them to Inflow AI.

Learn more from OpenAI's API data controls.

05. Services that process information

We work with service providers to operate specific app functions:

  • Apple: Sign in with Apple and App Store subscription purchases, renewals, and transaction handling. Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Google Firebase: Account authentication and storage of account-linked data such as subscription status and AI usage records. Firebase privacy information.
  • Cloudflare: Network infrastructure that routes and processes Inflow AI requests and related backend functions. Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • OpenAI: AI processing of content you submit through Inflow AI. OpenAI Privacy Policy.

We may also disclose information when legally required, to enforce applicable terms, or to protect the security and integrity of the service.

06. Storage and retention

Locally stored notebooks and documents remain on your device until you remove them or app data is deleted. Account identifiers, subscription records, and AI usage records may remain in our backend for as long as needed to operate your account, handle legitimate requests, comply with applicable law, or resolve disputes.

AI content submitted to external services is subject to those providers' retention and deletion practices, including security-related retention. Deleting local content does not necessarily delete information already processed or retained by a third-party service.

We review requests to delete account-associated information and remove data where we are able and legally permitted to do so. Some transaction or security records may need to be retained for legal or operational reasons.

07. Your privacy choices

  • Choose what to send to AI. You control whether to use Inflow AI and what questions, images, or page content you submit.
  • Manage local notes. You can edit, export, or delete notebook content on your device.
  • Manage subscriptions. Review or cancel Apple-billed subscriptions through your Apple Account subscription settings.
  • Use Apple's Hide My Email option. Sign in with Apple may let you share a private relay address instead of your personal address.
  • Request information or deletion. Contact us to ask about access, corrections, or deletion of account-associated data, subject to applicable legal requirements.

If you intend to remove Inflow, export important locally stored notebooks first. Deleting an app does not automatically cancel an Apple subscription.

08. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect information handled by Inflow, including secure connections for online services and access controls for backend account records. However, no network or storage system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

09. Age and children's privacy

Inflow is not directed to children under 13. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without an appropriate legal basis or required consent, we will take steps to address the situation. A parent or guardian with concerns may contact us using the details below.

10. International data processing

Inflow is developed in Canada. Our infrastructure and AI service providers may process information in other countries, including the United States, where privacy laws may differ from those in your location.

11. Updates to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the app, the services we use, or legal requirements. The date at the top of this page shows when the policy was last revised. Material changes will be communicated where required by law.
